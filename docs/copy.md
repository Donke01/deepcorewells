## 4. Process — the drilling journey

**Section kicker:** HOW IT HAPPENS
**Headline:** Five stages between you and water.

1. **Survey** — Before steel touches soil, we read the ground. A hydrogeological
   survey maps the aquifer so we drill where the water actually is — not where
   we hope it is.
2. **Drill** — Then the rig goes to work: metre by metre through soil and rock,
   casing the hole as it deepens, cuttings rising like smoke signals from below.
3. **Strike** — The moment every driller waits for. Water breaking through under
   pressure, flooding up the borehole. The strike.
4. **Pump** — A pump sized for your yield, set at depth, wired, and run-tested
   until the flow holds steady.
5. **Clean water** — We analyse the water, treat what needs treating, and leave
   you with a tap you can trust.
