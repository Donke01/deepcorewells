Why Boreholes Matter in Kenya

💧
Groundwater is one of Kenya's most reliable water sources, especially in arid and semi-arid regions. A properly drilled borehole provides a consistent, year-round supply — independent of rainfall and unreliable municipal systems.
Reliable Water Security
🌾
From smallholder farms to large-scale irrigation, boreholes power Kenyan agriculture. A borehole can transform a farm's productivity, enabling irrigation through dry seasons and supporting livestock, greenhouses, and horticulture.
Farming & Irrigation
🏡
A borehole is a long-term investment that adds value to homes, schools, hospitals, and businesses. It cuts water bills, ends dependence on water vendors, and — with proper drilling and licensing through WRA — serves for decades.
A Lasting Investment
