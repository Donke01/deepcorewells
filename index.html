How it happens

Five stages between you and water.

1
Surface

Survey

Before steel touches soil, we read the ground. A hydrogeological survey maps the aquifer so we drill where the water actually is — not where we hope it is.

2
Going down

Drill

Then the rig goes to work: metre by metre through soil and rock, casing the hole as it deepens, cuttings rising like smoke signals from below.

3
The breakthrough

Strike

The moment every driller waits for. Water breaking through under pressure, flooding up the borehole. The strike.

4
Set at depth

Pump

A pump sized for your yield, set at depth, wired, and run-tested until the flow holds steady.

5
First flow

Clean water

We analyse the water, treat what needs treating, and leave you with a tap you can trust.

Why a borehole

Why boreholes matter in Kenya

Reliable water security

Groundwater doesn't wait for rain. A properly drilled borehole delivers year-round, independent of rainfall and municipal supply.

Farming & irrigation

Boreholes carry Kenyan farms through the dry season: irrigation, livestock, greenhouses.

A lasting investment

Cut water bills, end vendor dependence, add value to the property. Licensed through WRA, a borehole serves for decades.
